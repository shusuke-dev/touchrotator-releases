Refunds

Try it free for 14 days first

Every feature of TouchRotator is free for 14 days, with nothing held back and nothing to pay. Use that time to confirm it works with your own touch panel.

After you buy

Because the trial lets you check first, we do not refund purchases as a rule. Please make sure it works on your own hardware while the trial is running.

If something does not work

Write to us before anything else. Most of it is settings (choosing the display to correct, running detect-by-tapping, calibrating). If it is not, and we can see why, we fix the app. info@tsubomi.jp

Exceptions

Purchases are processed by our merchant of record, who is the seller of record. Where their policy, or the law where you live, requires a refund, that applies, and it is handled through them.
