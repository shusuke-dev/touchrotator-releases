Privacy Policy

TouchRotator collects no personal information. Here is exactly what that means.

What the app does not collect

  • Your name, email address or any other personal detail
  • Touch coordinates, what you do with the app, or usage statistics
  • Your screen contents, or what is inside your apps and windows

Touch correction happens entirely on your Mac. Nothing about it leaves the machine.

When the app talks to the network

There is exactly one case:

  • Update check — the app asks GitHub's public API (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists. It sends an ordinary HTTP request with nothing that identifies you, and you can turn it off in settings.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Settings — rotation, the display being corrected, calibration values. Kept in macOS UserDefaults
  • Your licence key — kept in the macOS Keychain

Both stay on your Mac.

When you buy

Payment is handled by our merchant of record. They handle card details; we never receive them, and their privacy policy applies to that transaction. We learn only what is needed to issue a licence.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records access logs (including IP addresses). We use no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of our own.
