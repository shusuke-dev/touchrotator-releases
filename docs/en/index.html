What it does

Adding a touch monitor means having two screens or more. And the touch is not the only thing that goes wrong: which screen shows what, and where your windows end up every time you unplug. TouchRotator puts the whole set into one menu bar item.

1Touch that actually works

Out of the boxa finger can click, and nothing else
TouchRotatortap, scroll, right-click, drag

It reads the panel's USB signal (the HID digitizer) directly and turns your finger into those gestures. Where it can, it takes the panel exclusively, so macOS's own idea of which screen a touch should drive cannot get in the way. Your trackpad and mouse carry on as before.

A small offset from the display area is settled with 4-point calibration.

2Stand it on its side, and the pointer still lands where you touched

Out of the boxthe pointer sits away from your finger, or runs the other way
TouchRotatorthe pointer is under your finger

Turn the panel 90° and macOS still receives its coordinates in the old orientation. TouchRotator rotates them before passing them on, so a portrait panel behaves like any other. Rotation follows the macOS display setting on its own.

Using the panel landscape? Then you never need this. ⌥⌘R turns it on and off any time.

3The settings the extra screens bring, one click away

Out of the boxSystem Settings, then find the right screen
TouchRotatorone click from the menu bar

Resolution, brightness, mirroring and rotation, from the menu bar popup. One card per display, as many as you have plugged in. The screens themselves can be dragged into order in Settings → Displays.

Press a display's name and its card opens.

4Plug the monitor back in, get your windows back

Out of the boxthey stay piled on the built-in display
TouchRotatorback on the right screen, at the right size

Unplug a monitor and the windows that were on it are pushed onto the built-in display; plugging it back in does not bring them home. With two screens or more it happens most days. TouchRotator remembers where they were and puts them back.

Arrange things how you like them, then press Remember this layout once. From then on it happens by itself, and Restore layout brings it back any time.

If any of this sounds familiar
